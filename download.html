macOS builds

01

Which build?

Open the Apple menu → About This Mac. If the chip says Apple M1, M2, M3, M4 or later, take the Apple silicon build; if it says Intel, take the Intel build.

02

How to install

Open the downloaded .dmg, drag Taskstick into Applications, launch it, and pick your notes folder.
